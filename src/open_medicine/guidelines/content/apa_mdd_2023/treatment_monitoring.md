# Treatment Monitoring and Measurement-Based Care — APA 2023

## Overview

The APA Clinical Practice Guideline emphasizes the importance of systematic monitoring of treatment outcomes using validated measures. Measurement-based care (MBC) involves the routine use of standardized instruments to track symptom severity, guide treatment decisions, and evaluate progress. The guideline recommends using validated outcome measures throughout the treatment course for all three age cohorts.

## Recommended Outcome Measures

### Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9)

The PHQ-9 is the most widely used and recommended measure for depression screening and treatment monitoring in clinical practice.

| PHQ-9 Score | Severity | Suggested Action |
|---|---|---|
| **0-4** | Minimal depression | No treatment action required |
| **5-9** | Mild depression | Watchful waiting; repeat PHQ-9 at follow-up |
| **10-14** | Moderate depression | Treatment plan: consider counseling, follow-up, and/or pharmacotherapy |
| **15-19** | Moderately severe depression | Active treatment with pharmacotherapy and/or psychotherapy |
| **20-27** | Severe depression | Immediate initiation of pharmacotherapy; consider psychotherapy and/or referral to mental health specialist |

### Treatment Response Definitions

| Outcome | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Response** | 50% or greater reduction in PHQ-9 score from baseline |
| **Remission** | PHQ-9 follow-up score < 5 |
| **Partial response** | 25-49% reduction in PHQ-9 score from baseline |
| **Non-response** | Less than 25% reduction in PHQ-9 score from baseline |

Response is the preferable metric for comparing treatment outcomes because it does not favor more or less baseline symptom severity, indicates clinically meaningful improvement, and is transparent and easy to calculate.

## Monitoring Schedule

### Pharmacotherapy Monitoring

- **Weeks 1-2:** First follow-up to assess tolerability, address side effects, and ensure adherence
- **Weeks 4-6:** Assess treatment response using PHQ-9; if inadequate response, consider dose optimization
- **Weeks 6-8:** If no adequate response at therapeutic dose, reassess treatment plan (augment, switch, or add psychotherapy)
- **Monthly thereafter** until remission is sustained
- **Continuation phase:** Monitor every 1-3 months for 4-9 months after remission

### Psychotherapy Monitoring

- Assess using PHQ-9 within **6-12 weeks** of psychotherapy initiation, depending on the type of therapy
- Most evidence-based psychotherapies show meaningful symptom change by sessions 8-12
- If no improvement after an adequate course of psychotherapy (12-16 sessions), reassess treatment plan

### Adolescent Monitoring (Enhanced)

All antidepressants carry an FDA black-box warning regarding increased suicidality risk in patients under 25. Enhanced monitoring schedule:

- **Weekly** for the first 4 weeks after starting or changing medication
- **Biweekly** for weeks 5-8
- **At week 12** and as clinically indicated thereafter
- Monitor for clinical worsening, suicidality, or unusual behavioral changes
- Educate families and caregivers about the need for close observation

## Measurement-Based Care Algorithm

### Step 1: Baseline Assessment

- Administer PHQ-9 at initial evaluation
- Establish baseline severity score
- Document comorbid conditions, substance use, and prior treatment history
- If PHQ-9 >= 10, initiate treatment discussion with patient using shared decision-making

### Step 2: Treatment Initiation

- Select treatment modality based on severity, patient preference, and clinical factors:
  - PHQ-9 10-19 (moderate to moderately severe): psychotherapy OR pharmacotherapy
  - PHQ-9 >= 20 (severe): consider combined treatment or pharmacotherapy with close monitoring
  - PHQ-9 5-9 (mild): watchful waiting with active monitoring; consider treatment if symptoms are persistent or functionally impairing

### Step 3: Early Follow-Up (Weeks 2-4)

- Repeat PHQ-9
- Assess medication tolerability and adherence (if pharmacotherapy)
- Assess engagement and attendance (if psychotherapy)
- If significant side effects or safety concerns, modify treatment immediately

### Step 4: Response Assessment (Weeks 6-8)

- Repeat PHQ-9 and calculate percent change from baseline
- **If response (>=50% reduction):** continue current treatment; monitor for remission
- **If partial response (25-49% reduction):** optimize dose (pharmacotherapy) or increase session frequency; reassess at 4 weeks
- **If non-response (<25% reduction):** implement treatment change:
  - Add psychotherapy to pharmacotherapy (or vice versa)
  - Switch antidepressant
  - Augment with additional medication

### Step 5: Remission and Continuation

- **Target:** PHQ-9 < 5 (remission)
- Once remission achieved, continue same treatment for 4-9 months (continuation phase)
- For patients with two or more prior episodes, longer maintenance phase is recommended
- Monitor for relapse during continuation and maintenance phases

### Step 6: Relapse Prevention

- Before discontinuation, counsel patients about relapse potential
- Establish a concrete plan for recognizing symptom recurrence and re-engaging in treatment
- Consider psychotherapy-based relapse prevention (CBT, IPT, or MBCT) for patients with recurrent MDD
- Monitor for several months after treatment discontinuation

## Suicidality Assessment

- PHQ-9 item 9 specifically asks about thoughts of self-harm or being better off dead
- **Any score > 0 on item 9 requires immediate clinical assessment** for suicidal ideation, plan, intent, and access to means
- A positive item 9 does not automatically indicate acute suicidality but mandates further evaluation
- Document the assessment and safety plan in the medical record

## Special Monitoring Considerations

### Older Adults (Age 60+)

- Monitor for anticholinergic burden (cognitive effects, urinary retention, constipation)
- Assess for falls risk, particularly with medications causing orthostatic hypotension
- Monitor QTc if prescribing citalopram (maximum 20 mg/day in patients over 60)
- Consider cognitive screening (e.g., MoCA) to distinguish depression from neurodegenerative disease
- Assess functional status and social isolation as both risk factors and treatment targets

### Children and Adolescents

- Use age-appropriate measures in addition to PHQ-9 (PHQ-A for adolescents)
- Involve parents/caregivers in monitoring and treatment decisions
- Enhanced suicidality monitoring per FDA requirements (see above)
- Monitor academic and social functioning as markers of treatment response

## Limitations

- The guideline recommends measurement-based care but acknowledges that implementation in routine practice remains inconsistent
- PHQ-9 is a self-report measure and may be influenced by response bias, literacy level, and cultural factors
- Specific monitoring intervals are informed by clinical practice standards and expert consensus rather than rigorous comparative effectiveness research
- The optimal frequency and method of monitoring across diverse clinical settings has not been definitively established
- The guideline does not specify cutoff scores for every treatment decision; clinical judgment remains essential alongside standardized measures
