# Interpretation of 10-Year Risk — ACC/AHA 2013

## Risk Categories and Thresholds

The 10-year ASCVD risk score is utilized in shared decision-making to guide primary prevention strategies, particularly the initiation of statin therapy. Based on the 2013 ACC/AHA blood cholesterol guidelines and subsequent clinical practice statements, the calculated percentage is generally interpreted across four clinical categories:

- **< 5.0%:** Low Risk. Emphasize lifestyle optimization.
- **5.0% to < 7.5%:** Borderline Risk. Consider primary prevention strategies and evaluation of risk-enhancing factors.
- **7.5% to < 20.0%:** Intermediate Risk. Statin therapy consideration is indicated. (A 10-year risk of ≥ 7.5% is the classic threshold identified for moderate- or high-intensity statin benefit).
- **≥ 20.0%:** High Risk. Statin therapy is definitely recommended.

## Implementation Notes

- The primary threshold explicitly linked to the 2013 ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equations for establishing clear statin benefit is a **10-year ASCVD risk of ≥ 7.5%**.
- These categorizations are intended for primary prevention in patients without established clinical ASCVD.
- Clinical judgment and patient preference (shared decision-making) remain central to the application of these risk thresholds.
